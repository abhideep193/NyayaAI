# NyayaAI

Plain-language legal information for people in India, in English, Hindi or Hinglish.
Answers come **only** from the official text of 11 Indian Acts (India Code): copyright,
trade marks, patents, and criminal law (BNS, BNSS, IT Act, POCSO, POSH, Domestic Violence,
Dowry Prohibition, SC/ST Atrocities). Every quote in an answer is checked word for word
against the Act; quotes that cannot be found are flagged.

NyayaAI gives legal information, not legal advice. Questions are sent to OpenAI to write the
answer; do not include names or personal details.

This demo asks for an access code because every question is paid for by the developer.
