## 14. [contract] Is a verbal agreement legally valid in India, or does it have to be in writing?

**Confidence:** high (model said: high)  
**Expected sections:** contract_act_1872:10  
**Automatic result:** PASS  
**Time:** 9.8 s

### Answer

Yes. A verbal agreement can be legally valid in India; an agreement does not always have to be in writing.

Generally, to be a contract, an agreement must have the free consent of people legally capable of contracting, a lawful purpose and lawful consideration—something done, given up or promised in return. It must also not be expressly declared void. [contract_act_1872:10] [contract_act_1872:2]

However, some contracts must be in writing, witnessed or registered if another applicable law requires it. Whether a particular verbal agreement is enforceable depends on its facts and the applicable requirements. [contract_act_1872:10]
